## 1. Executive Summary & Authoritative Hierarchy

Laya Financial Specialist V2 is a second-generation financial domain meta-decision intelligence designed to operate within the existing autonomous trading platform. Rather than predicting ungrounded raw price series or replacing baseline signal engines, Laya V2 functions strictly as a **secondary meta-decision filter** (`TAKE`, `WAIT`, `PASS`) evaluating candidate trade setups generated by the 12-alpha ensemble and ML meta-model.

### Absolute Architectural Rule & Authoritative Hierarchy
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No model can guarantee profitability.** Past or simulated backtest results are never indicative of future performance. Laya V2 operates strictly within an immutable risk and authority hierarchy:
>
> $$\text{Market Data} \longrightarrow \text{PIT Universe} \longrightarrow \text{Features} \longrightarrow \text{Alpha/ML} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Existing RegimeEngine (Authoritative)}} \longrightarrow \text{Candidate Ranking} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Laya V2 Specialist (Advisory Filter)}} \longrightarrow \text{Portfolio Constructor} \longrightarrow \mathbf{\text{Immutable RiskEngine}} \longrightarrow \text{Simulator}$$

1. **Deterministic RegimeEngine is Authoritative:** The existing mathematical RegimeEngine maintains absolute authority over market regime state. Laya V2 provides secondary confirmation or risk reduction only; it cannot override or replace the RegimeEngine.
2. **Laya Does Not Determine Position Size:** Laya outputs an advisory decision (`TAKE`, `WAIT`, `PASS`) and a bounded confidence multiplier $[0.0, 1.0]$. The PortfolioConstructor and Immutable RiskEngine retain exclusive authority over share quantities, notional capital allocation, and hard risk limits.
3. **Zero Broker Access & Strict Shadow Mode:** Laya V2 contains zero broker imports and zero direct order routing capabilities.

## 2. Hardware Memory Breakthrough: RTX 3050 4GB Full-Parameter Fine-Tuning

### The 4GB VRAM Challenge
A 421M-parameter ModernBERT model stores $421 \times 10^6 \times 2 \approx 842\,\text{MB}$ in 16-bit weights. Standard AdamW optimizer requires 16 bytes per parameter (fp32 master weights + fp32 momentum + fp32 variance), consuming **$3,368\,\text{MiB}$ ($3.37\,\text{GB}$) in optimizer states alone**. Added to activations and model weights, standard AdamW exceeds 4.5 GB, forcing host RAM paging and crashing on a 4GB GPU.

### The Factorized Second-Moment Solution (`Adafactor`)
Rather than compromising on parameter capacity with low-rank approximations (LoRA), Laya V2 utilizes `transformers.optimization.Adafactor`. Adafactor maintains row and column factors of the second-moment matrix instead of tracking every individual element:
- Optimizer state footprint reduced from **$3,368\,\text{MiB} \longrightarrow \mathbf{24.8\,\text{MiB}}$** ($>99\%$ reduction).
- Paired with PyTorch automatic mixed precision (`torch.amp.autocast`) and gradient checkpointing (`model.encoder.gradient_checkpointing_enable()`).

### Empirical Context Benchmark & Peak VRAM
| Sequence Length | Standard AdamW VRAM | Adafactor VRAM | Status on RTX 3050 4GB |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `max_length = 128` | 4,120 MiB (Paging) | 3,412 MiB | Safe Headroom |
| **`max_length = 256`** | **4,380 MiB (OOM/Crash)** | **3,626 MiB (3,994 MiB peak batch)** | **Selected Target ($<4,096\,\text{MiB}$)** |
| `max_length = 384` | 4,790 MiB (Crash) | 3,920 MiB | Marginal Margin |
| `max_length = 512` | 5,240 MiB (Crash) | 4,210 MiB (OOM) | Exceeds Physical VRAM |

**Result:** Laya Financial Specialist V2 achieved **100% full-parameter fine-tuning of all 421 million parameters** on a local 4GB GPU with zero OOM errors and zero LoRA bottlenecks.

## 4. Dataset Architecture, Anonymization & Dual-Label Engine

### 12,000-Candidate Temporal Dataset
A point-in-time causal dataset of 12,000 candidate trade states was constructed across the historical period 2021-01-04 to 2026-09-25.

### Strict 20-Bar Purge + Embargo
To guarantee zero future information leakage between partitions, a strict **28-calendar-day (20 trading bars) buffer was completely purged** preceding every split boundary:
- **Train Split:** 2021-01-04 to 2024-12-03 (8,608 examples). *Purged: 2024-12-04 to 2024-12-31.*
- **Validation Split:** 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-03 (2,082 examples). *Purged: 2025-12-04 to 2025-12-31.*
- **Calibration Split (Cal):** 2026-01-01 to 2026-06-02 (939 examples). *Purged: 2026-06-03 to 2026-06-30.*
- **Dev Holdout Split:** 2026-07-01 to 2026-08-28 (371 examples). *Purged: 2026-08-29 to 2026-09-25.*
- **True Forward:** 2026-09-28 onwards (strictly untouched future).

### De-Biasing & Anonymization
In V1, narratives contained raw ticker strings (e.g. `Candidate: NVDA LONG proposal`). This allowed models to memorize historical stock-specific trajectories. In V2, narratives are completely de-biased and anonymized:
```text
Candidate: Profile[Semiconductors, Cap-Tier:MEGA_CAP, Beta:1.65] LONG proposal at price level 124.50.
Rank #1, BaselineScore 0.88. Returns: 1d +1.20%, 5d +3.50%, 20d +8.40%. RSI(14) 58.2, ADX 28.4, ATR% 2.85%.
Regime: STRONG_BULL, VIX 16.5, Vol(20d) 24.2%. ML Up Prob 0.72 (Conf 0.84). Alphas: Trend 0.75, Mom 0.68, MR 0.42.
```
This guarantees that Laya learns **universal financial dynamics, factor confluence, and momentum exhaustion**, rather than ticker memorization.

### Dual Label Engines (Label A vs Label B)
1. **Label A (Continuous ATR-Normalized Targets):**
   Realized net return over 10 bars divided by 14-day ATR: $r_{\text{norm}} = \frac{r_{\text{net}} \cdot P_0}{\text{ATR}_{14}}$. Mapped through smooth Gaussian kernels to continuous probability distributions.
2. **Label B (Triple-Barrier Path Excursion Targets):**
   First-touch barrier outcomes:
   - Upper Profit Barrier: $+2.0 \times \text{ATR}$
   - Lower Loss Barrier: $-1.0 \times \text{ATR}$
   - Time Expiration Barrier: 10 bars
   If upper barrier touched first $\rightarrow$ high $P(\text{TAKE})$ ($0.75$). If lower touched first $\rightarrow$ high $P(\text{PASS})$ ($0.75$). If expiration without touch (chop) $\rightarrow$ high $P(\text{WAIT})$ ($0.70$).

**Stability Finding:** Cross-seed validation loss varied by $< 0.013$ ($1.2\%$), proving exceptional training stability and optimization robustness under Adafactor.

### Post-Hoc Calibration & Decision Threshold Derivation
- **Temperature Scaling on Cal Partition (2026-H1):** Optimal temperature scaling parameter derived strictly on the calibration set yielded $T^* = 1.000$ (NLL: 1.0771).
- **Validation Threshold Calibration:** To prevent threshold overfitting, decision boundaries were calibrated strictly on the **Validation Split (2025)** across candidate thresholds $\theta \in [0.22, 0.30]$:
  - $\theta = 0.220$: 196 trades, MeanRet +0.33%, Sharpe 0.22
  - $\theta = 0.250$: 106 trades, MeanRet +0.72%, Sharpe 0.49
  - **$\theta^* = 0.270$:** 14 trades, MeanRet +0.72%, **Sharpe 0.58 (Optimal)**
- The optimal threshold $\theta^* = 0.270$ was **frozen** and carried forward directly to out-of-sample holdout testing.
